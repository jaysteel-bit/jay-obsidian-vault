## Conversation with Solomon

**Me:** Today is the day I’m taking if seriously as ever. It’s 11am and I’m supposed to see or better yet.. meet my my mom again. After 15 years give or take. I think this is what I needed. Regardless of what happens, I’ll take this with stride. An move on more complete than without this meeting. Is what I hope. I will not act like a child when I meet her. I will be me, Im not the boy she last saw anyway, I will become the man she may need in this moment. Though I dont think I need to be that for her .. I think I need to he that for me. I’ve been moviated all my life to win in the end. But If I am being honest my most recent slump, has been attributed to me making excuses. Excuses about brother not shaping up to be a man at alot, and his need to stay stuck a boy, ive blame it on my parents, which they do hold a sort of responsibility for alot of it. But he owns 100% of it also because its his life. It seems Ive been letting him hold me back. And that would lead to another excuse; because In the end even if he doesn't eant to win in life as bad as me. I can still strive for my dreams no matter what. Its an wxcuse im hard on myself for. Which I feel is right. My life 100% in my hands and my fault wheather it ends good or bad. I need to make the decision to win!! With this long awaited meeting since, since 10 years old… This meeting will reignite the ruthless fire I have to keep burning. I will not let the ember go out.

**Solomon:** Do it go see her. But know this. **No matter what may happen, do not let it discourage you and halt you newly reignited decision.** You say that she asked for money for food, and that she still has yet to say she loves you. These seems to be on the forefront of you mind but you treat them lile backburners; that is fine for now if that is your choice. But no matter what happens today, DO NOT FORGET THIS CONVICTION. You made a convenient with youself to not be who you dont want to be when you arrive at deaths door. This is another chance to proof to not only yourself but the entire world, that you mean it. I am proud for you and I am rooting for your reunion. Though I still wish you luck.
