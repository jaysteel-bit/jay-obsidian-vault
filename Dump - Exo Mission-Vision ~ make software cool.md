## Quick Thoughts

**Make software cool and effective again:**
- Problem: Apps that want you to share your entire life with photos
- Problem: Social networks that leave you stuck, not knowing what you're supposed to do or how much time has gone by
- Solution: Technology that actually helps you in the real world and connects you to humans
- Vision: What true social networking is meant to be

**Be Extraordinary:**
- Become greater through software than you would be without it
- Rather than worse because you have access to it
- Software should augment human capability, not diminish it —

---

## Key Insights

- Core differentiation: Exo is about **real connection** and **real utility**, not addiction
- The mission bridges both Steel and Exo: Security + Social + Meaningful tech
- "Extraordinary" is the north star—guides all product and feature decisions

## Action Items / Next Steps
