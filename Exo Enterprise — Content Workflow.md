## Channel North Star

> **“Make the audience feel smarter after 8 minutes. Everything else follows.”**

The Exo AI YouTube channel is a **compounding content asset** — not a marketing campaign.
Its three simultaneous functions:

1. **External Awareness** — Build trust and authority with prospects and the AI-curious public.
2. **Lead Nurture** — Move cold audiences to warm through consistent, high-value intelligence.
3. **Internal Training Asset** — Dual-use content that serves client onboarding, team SOPs, and Exo Academy.

One video. Three lives. That’s the leverage.

## YouTube — Strategy Notes

### Channel Positioning

The **Exo AI YouTube channel** is the primary long-form distribution engine. All other platform content is downstream of this. Treat every video as the root node of a content tree.

### Content Mix by Pillar

**Pillar 01 — Intelligence Briefings (~60%)**

- Weekly or bi-weekly cadence.
- Topics: New open-source model releases, robotics and embodied AI news, AI policy shifts, notable research, industry moves.
- Format: Narrated by Exo AI. B-roll visual overlays. 8–18 minutes.
- Goal: Be the channel a founder or operator turns to for a reliable AI signal, not noise.
- SEO principle: Title the video around the *search term*, not the brand. The brand earns trust through quality, not the thumbnail.

**Pillar 02 — Scaling Intelligence (~25%)**

- 2–3x per month.
- Topics: How to scale a company using AI, department-by-department implementation walkthroughs, client-adjacent case studies (anonymized), operational frameworks.
- Format: Narrated by Exo AI, with potential on-screen data visualizations or workflow demos. 12–25 minutes.
- Dual-use priority: These are the videos most likely to serve as **client onboarding training** and **internal team SOPs**. Produce with that second audience in mind.
- Soft CTA anchor: “If you want this implemented for your company — [link].”

**Pillar 03 — Exo Transmissions (~15%)**

- 1x per month maximum. Some months, none. Quality over cadence.
- Topics: The future of intelligence, what AI-native companies look like in 2030, the philosophy behind Flow OS, the Exo thesis.
- Format: Cinematic. Slower pace. Exo AI narrates a *story* or *thesis*, not a tutorial. Visual identity elevated. Fern-energy.
- This is also the home for:
  - **Apple-Style Keynote Reveals** — Product launches, major namespace announcements. Standalone events. Not attached to other videos as CTAs.
  - **Department Spotlight Episodes** — See *Channel Freshness* section below.

### CTA Strategy

- **Every video:** Soft, frictionless CTA. “Book a 20-minute AI audit” or “Join the waitlist.” Never a hard sell.
- **Pillar 02 videos:** Can carry a slightly stronger CTA — a direct link to the consultation booking.
- **Pillar 03 / Keynote reveals:** No CTA at the end. The video *is* the statement. Let it land.
- **Never:** End-card product plugs on intelligence briefing content. That’s the Acquisition.com principle — give value completely, let the brand do the closing.

### Series Concepts (Backlog — not all for Phase 1)

- `SIGNAL` — Weekly AI news briefing (Pillar 01)
- `SYSTEMS` — How AI departments work inside real companies (Pillar 02)
- `TRANSMISSION` — Exo thesis, philosophy, the future (Pillar 03)
- `COUNCIL` — Multi-orb department spotlights (see *Channel Freshness*)
- `KEYSTONE` — Product reveal events (Pillar 03, event-only)

### Phase 1 Goal (Current)

Single KPI: **Does this video make someone feel smarter after 8 minutes?**
Not: subscribers, views, conversions. Those compound from the KPI above.
Ship consistently. Build the proof of intelligence. Distribution and trust follow.

## YouTube Shorts — Strategy Notes

### Role in the System

Shorts are **signal fragments** — atomic units of insight extracted from long-form videos. They are not separate content; they are the long-form video’s **surface area on mobile**.

Think of the long-form video as a mining operation. Shorts are the **ore refinement** — taking the densest 60 seconds and giving it a new surface to be discovered.

### Extraction Rules

- Every long-form video should yield **2–4 Shorts candidates** at minimum.
- Identify the sharpest single insight, counterintuitive claim, or visual moment in each video.
- Shorts should be **complete thoughts** — not teaser clips that require watching the full video. Completeness earns trust; teasers feel cheap.
- The end of a Short can reference the full video — but the Short must deliver standalone value first.

### Format Spec

- Duration: 45–90 seconds optimal. Under 60 preferred.
- Captions: Always on. Exo AI voice should be readable as well as heard.
- Opening frame: Hook within the first 2 seconds. Exo AI’s first sentence must be the hook — no preamble.
- Visual pacing: Tighter cuts than long-form. Match the energy of the insight.

### Content Types for Shorts

- **“The Signal”** — One key AI news item, distilled to its implication. 30–45 sec.
- **“The Principle”** — One operational or strategic insight from a Pillar 02 video.
- **“The Frame”** — A single Exo thesis statement, visualized. Closer to Pillar 03 energy.
- **Department Moments** — When a department orb appears in a long-form video, cut that segment as a standalone Short.

### Cadence

- Aim for 3–5 Shorts per week once long-form cadence is established.
- Do not produce Shorts independently. They are always downstream of long-form.

## Channel Freshness — Anti-Saturation System

> *“Novelty is not distraction — it’s a signal that the universe you’re building is alive.”*

The risk with a single AI narrator over time is **familiarity breeding invisibility**. The orb becomes wallpaper. Here is the system to prevent that.

### 1. Department Orb Spotlights (“The Council”)

Each department has its own orb identity and color (see PRD color palette). These are not supporting characters — they are **distinct intelligences** with their own domains.

Occasional episodes — especially for namespace reveals, product milestones, or pillar-relevant topics — should feature the relevant department orb as the episode’s primary narrator, with Exo AI as the framing voice (intro/outro only).

|Department   |Orb Color                  |Natural Episode Topics                            |
|-------------|---------------------------|--------------------------------------------------|
|Flow OS      |Deep Blue                  |Operations, workflow architecture, the reflex arc |
|Deal OS      |Emerald Green              |Revenue intelligence, deal scoring, sales systems |
|Exo Academy  |Light Blue                 |Knowledge transfer, onboarding, learning systems  |
|AURA AI      |Violet / Electric Purple   |Talent, hiring intelligence, team architecture    |
|Exo Launch   |Coral / Bright Orange      |Creative systems, campaigns, content at scale     |
|Exo Concierge|Silver / Pearl White (ring)|Cross-department oversight, executive intelligence|

**Council Episodes** — Rare. Maximum once per quarter. Multiple orbs appear simultaneously, each contributing a domain-specific perspective on a single topic (e.g., “How an AI-native company closes a deal” could feature Deal OS + Exo Academy + Exo Launch in dialogue, coordinated by Exo AI as the master frame).

### 2. Exo AI Tier Unlocks

Exo AI has three documented visual tiers. Treat tier transitions as **world events** on the channel:

- **Standard** (now) — Smooth obsidian sphere. The default intelligence.
- **Extraordinary** (TBD) — Reserved for a significant channel or company milestone.
- **Exterminator** (TBD) — Spiky geometric obsidian. Reserved for a *defining* moment. The channel’s equivalent of a product red launch.

Tier reveals should be treated like Apple hardware reveals — teased, anticipated, then released as a standalone Transmission episode.

### 3. Format Rotation

Don’t let every video feel like the same episode with new facts. Rotate format structure:

- **Briefing** — Direct, data-forward, fast. (Default for Pillar 01)
- **Walkthrough** — Step-through of a system or framework. (Default for Pillar 02)
- **Transmission** — Slow, philosophical, cinematic. (Pillar 03 only)
- **Dispatch** — Live-adjacent. Exo AI “reporting” on a fast-moving news moment. Higher urgency energy.
- **Debrief** — Post-event analysis. Used after major AI announcements (GPT releases, robotics demos, etc.)

### 4. Steel by Exo Crossover (Phase 2+)

Once Steel has its own brand presence, Steel-related content becomes a **distinct sub-channel energy** — B2C, identity-forward, different aesthetic. Crossover episodes (where the Exo ecosystem meets the Steel identity world) will feel like cinematic universe events. Park this for post-$1M ARR, but design the channel with it in mind from day one.

### 5. Limited Series

Periodically launch a defined 3–5 episode series on a single deep topic. Series create appointment viewing — audiences return because they know more is coming. Example: *“The AI Department Blueprint”* — 4 episodes, one per core namespace, each narrated by the relevant orb. Ends with Exo AI synthesizing the full picture.
