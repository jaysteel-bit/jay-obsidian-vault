Structuring ExoCare Safely

Your ideal client's biggest fear during the "Transfer" phase is that **the AI workflows will break, API integrations will deprecate, or the team will fail to manage the prompt/model drift**, dragging the founder back into the operational bottleneck.

You do not need an insurance company for this. You are insuring them with your **technical expertise and labor**, not cash payouts.

The Legal Framework for your Contract

In your master services agreement, position ExoCare as a **Warranty and Maintenance SLA**.

- **What you promise:** A guarantee of system uptime, prompt optimization, and immediate bug fixes if an AI workflow breaks.
- **How you limit liability:** State that ExoCare covers the _remediation_ of the software and workflows to their original operating state. Explicitly state that ExoCare does not indemnify the client for "consequential damages" (e.g., lost revenue if an AI sales bot misses a lead).

Rewriting the Marketing Message for the OTO

You can position ExoCare as the ultimate peace-of-mind safety net without using illegal insurance language.

- **The "Insurance" Trap (Avoid this):** _"For $10K/year, ExoCare insures your systems. If a workflow breaks and you lose a client, our insurance covers your financial damages up to X."_
- **The "AppleCare" Framework (Use this):** _"When you transition to independent ownership, the biggest risk is system drift. ExoCare is your enterprise-grade warranty. If an API updates, a prompt breaks, or LLM latency spikes, our engineering team deploys automatically to rebuild and patch the workflow at zero hourly cost to you. We keep your AI operating system running exactly as designed."_

Value-Add for the ExoCare Retainer

To make the $6K–$12K/year price point a no-brainer, bundle the warranty with high-margin, low-labor maintenance:

- **Monthly Prompt Tuning:** Keeping their LLM costs low and outputs sharp.

- **Exo Academy Access:** Keeping their internal team trained on new Flow OS features.

## 2. Limitation of Liability Clause (Contract Draft)

This language protects Exo Enterprise LLC from catastrophic lawsuits if an AI model drifts or an API breaks, while keeping the client focused on the "repair" mechanism. Insert this into your Master Services Agreement (MSA) or ExoCare Addendum:

> LIMITATION OF LIABILITY & EXCLUSION OF DAMAGES
> 
> (a) Maximum Aggregate Liability. To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, the total aggregate liability of Exo Enterprise LLC ("Exo") arising out of or related to the ExoCare program, whether in contract, tort (including negligence), strict liability, or otherwise, shall be strictly limited to the total amount of fees actually paid by Client to Exo for the specific ExoCare subscription tier during the twelve (12) month period immediately preceding the event giving rise to liability.
> 
> (b) Exclusion of Consequential Damages. In no event shall Exo be liable to Client or any third party for any indirect, incidental, special, exemplary, punitive, or consequential damages whatsoever. This includes, without limitation, lost profits, lost revenue, loss of data, loss of business reputation, or operational downtime, even if Exo has been advised of the possibility of such damages.
> 
> (c) Remedy Limitation. Client acknowledges that Exo’s sole obligation, and Client’s exclusive remedy under the ExoCare warranty, is the technical remediation of the covered Flow OS workflows and AI architectures to their original operational specifications. If Exo fails to restore a covered workflow within seventy-two (72) consecutive hours of a validated Severity 1 ticket, Client's sole and exclusive financial remedy is a credit equal to one (1) month of their current ExoCare service fee, subject to the conditions of the ExoCare Guarantee.
