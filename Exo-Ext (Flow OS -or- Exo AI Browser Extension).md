EXO-ENT Brainstorm:

Exo Ext is a browser extension that brings your Flow OS "Department-in-a-Box" ecosystem directly into the user's daily workflow, acting as an intelligent capture and command layer for AI-powered operations. Rather than requiring users to context-switch to a separate application, Exo Ext embeds itself as a sidebar or side panel in Chromium-based browsers (Chrome, Edge, Brave, Arc), allowing users to interact with Exo AI while browsing, capture visual context from any tab using screenshot APIs, and trigger department-specific agents based on what they're doing. The extension connects to your Flow OS backend via standard API calls, sending captured context (screenshots, selected text, URLs) to your AI systems and displaying responses inline, maintaining the diffs-based architecture that powers your reflex arc engine.

The flagship feature is SOP Capture—a real-time workflow documentation system that leverages Chrome's tabCapture and desktopCapture APIs to record users' screens and audio as they perform processes. Users click "Start SOP Recording," perform their workflow while narrating their steps in natural language, then click "Stop." The extension sends the recording to your backend, where multimodal AI (Claude 3.5 Sonnet, Grok, others or user choice) processes the video frames and audio transcription to generate clean, structured SOPs with numbered steps, screenshots, and clear instructions. This transforms messy, ad-hoc knowledge into documented, repeatable processes that survive team turnover—directly addressing one of your ICP's biggest pain points.

What makes Exo Ext strategically powerful is its department-aware intelligence. The extension detects which Flow OS namespace the user is operating in—based on the URL, tab content, or user selection—and formats outputs accordingly. If you're in a CRM (Deal OS context), it generates sales process SOPs with deal stages; if you're in a project management tool (Flow OS context), it creates workflow templates; if you're handling customer support (Support OS context), it builds support playbooks or makes sure to focus on Customer Success context. If your onboarding/training new hire it can use EXA to keep them up to speed within their tabs, (ETC.) ... Every captured SOP automatically creates diffs in your eternal schema (namespace:academy, entity_type:sop, event:created , etc.), feeding your data gravity moat with proprietary human judgment and workflow knowledge. The extension isn't just a recording tool—it's the front door to your entire BOMT (Build-Operate-Manage-Transfer) delivery model, capturing institutional knowledge that makes clients increasingly dependent on your ecosystem.

Your "Middleware" Strategy
Your positioning as a catch-all assistant that plugs into frontier models is smart for several reasons:

Capital efficiency: You avoid the $100M+ required to train competitive foundation models
Flexibility: Users get best-of-breed models (Claude, GPT-4, Gemini) through one interface
Defensibility: Your moat becomes the workflow data and department-specific "reflex arcs" you capture, not the underlying LLM
This aligns perfectly with your Flow OS thesis about learning from user workflows and building data defensibility through structured diffs

The business model follows your capability-stack licensing approach (may need to think pricing over once more). The Self-Service Tier provides the extension for $20-50/user/month, allowing teams to record and generate their own SOPs autonomously. The Exo Concierge Tier is the high-touch upsell: your team schedules professional documentation sessions, interviews multiple stakeholders, produces multi-format SOPs (video, PDF, interactive playbooks), and provides quality assurance—priced at $500-2,000+ per SOP or via retainer. This mirrors your BOM/T hybrid model perfectly: start with software (the extension), layer in services (Concierge), and create compounding value through Exo Academy integration where all captured SOPs feed into your knowledge synthesis engine which will be connected throughout all departments.
