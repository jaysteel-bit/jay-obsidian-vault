#### **Perplexity Research:**

Air.inc is a creative operations platform that merges digital asset management with AI-powered tagging, visual collaboration, and generative content multiplication. By analyzing its feature set, Exo Launch can evolve beyond a basic storage tool into a comprehensive, autonomous creative department within Flow OS.[1][2]

## Intelligent Asset Management
Air utilizes its AI engine, Paige, to automatically tag uploads with facial, object, and text recognition capabilities. It automatically transcribes videos upon upload, generating searchable spoken dialogue, smart summaries, and timeline chapters. Replicating this means Exo Launch users can use natural language to instantly find exact moments in raw footage without relying on perfect folder structures.[3][4][5][6]

## Visual Collaboration Workflows
Air centralizes creative feedback by allowing stakeholders to pin time-stamped comments directly onto video frames and specific areas of images. The platform routes these assets through Kanban boards that track version history and strict approval statuses. For Exo Launch, integrating this visual proofing with the Flow OS Diffs Engine will automatically capture the human reasoning behind every creative revision.[7][8][9][1]

## Automated Content Multiplication
The platform enables teams to take one approved asset and automatically resize, reformat, and adapt it for multiple channels. It features generative AI editing and connects to over 50 embedded image models to scale campaigns instantly. Building this multiplication engine into Exo Launch directly supports your goal of increasing content output tenfold while enforcing strict brand guidelines.[10][1][3][7]

## Seamless External Distribution
Air streamlines client interactions using frictionless upload forms that automatically organize incoming files without requiring a login. It distributes final deliverables through customizable, presentation-ready visual boards secured by granular permission settings. Implementing similar external portals in Exo Launch will eliminate scattered email threads and simplify the asset handoff process.[4][8][7]
