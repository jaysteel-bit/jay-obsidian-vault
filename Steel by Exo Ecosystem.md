## Quick Thoughts

## Steel by Exo (Braindump)

What it is: Consumer-facing sub-brand of Exo Enterprise — a premium identity ecosystem built around physical NFC artifacts + digital identity. Exo builds the engine; Steel is the luxury identity product line.

**The v0.1 chain**: *Steel Card (Digital First/Physical Later) → Steel Profile → Steel Global Citizenship → World Participation*

Products (confirmed):

- **Steel Card** — NFC metal card (digital version first), application/invitation-only (physical app NFC tap to share mechanism increase TAM via Viral-ity mechanism), first 10K waitlist, Q4 2026 target. No visible numbers/chips/stripes. Zero static data(if worth it), dynamic tokens, AES-128/256 encryption. 304 stainless steel, brushed gunmetal design maybe, if luxury makes sense.

- **Steel App** — I see alot of leverage here. Mobile-first identity. Steel Card within. NFC tap-to-share/network, steel.id link, analytics dashboard, context-aware profile modes (Professional / Creative / Network / Public). Also we can incorporate Steel Products NFC taps in the future, where if our products are tapped information in app comes up about it along with the rarity or brand culture or something of the product. **Main Flow: “#Tap to Connect #####Simulate STEEL tap-to-share 
[green ‘stimulate tap’ button]” After tap, connection successful (saves to history for later viewing if public or post pin passed) — Pin Required [if password protected—prompts you for your passcode / Face ID if enabled]; if public mode then goes straight to profile to begin connection. [Visual Mock Up On @steel.html or @STEEL PAGE]

- **Steel Profile (steel.id)** — Verified digital identity / Linktree killer. Targets creators, founders, professionals. Differentiated by verified membership weight + NFC bridge + Steel Global integration. **The core idea: profile has your social networks + key routes (email icon/real estate number [work phone icon]/personal phone number icon/instagram icon/twitter or X icon/Linked In icon/ YouTube icon/etc..): that helps individuals (B2C) connect instantly. Above the social links scrollable row is your profile picture. At the bottom of the vertical profile card is “Add to Contacts (or similar)” — with later AI Features coming: at the very bottom AI thin announcement bar designed chat box with microphone at the far left. Obviously so we can incorporate AI in the future of this social ecosystem. 
[Visual Mock Up On @steel.html or @STEEL PAGE]

- **Steel for Teams** — Enterprise/agency tier. B2B adjacent.

- **Steel Bracelet/Wearables** — Modular NFC wearable (Phase 2+). Core module + interchangeable bands (sport silicone, fashion leather, jewelry metal mesh, fabric NATO). Flagship Veblen item. Currently deferred — card-first.

- Steel Ecomm — Streetwear (Hoodies, sweat suits, etc) / Veblen goods / etc.. — currently underdeveloped 
  

Brand Architecture:

- Steel (Standard) — Accessible entry

- Steel+ (Premium) — Enhanced features, higher-grade materials 

- Steel++ (Luxury/Veblen) — Extremely limited flagship items that anchor prestige

**The Above Tiers is a brainstorming experience**

Revenue Model (not finalized):

DTC physical products, digital subscription (App/steel.id premium), Steel Global membership, B2B licensing.

Punchline: Steel is not a tech startup — it's a premium identity brand. The card is the acquisition event; the profile and membership are the retention moat. The Linktree disruption isn't a side feature — it's the entry wedge.

Steel Global

What it is: The community/culture layer of the Steel ecosystem — "curating the unimagineable." A polymathic membership universe where each "world" is a distinct vertical (Business, Fashion, Culture, AI, Cinema, Photography, Design, Futurism, Venture, etc.) with its own editorial voice and community.

  

Website: steelglobal.us (live, early stage). The Foundry: steelglobal.us/foundry.

  

How it works:

  

- Worlds — not feeds. Curated editorial + community environments where taste is the filter.

- Steel Citizenship — earned tier system: World Member → World Contributor → World Featured → World Editor. Earned, not purchased.

- Steel Tag — member attribution. Content tied to Steel Profile, not social handle. Reputation builds on verified identity, not follower count.

- Content 3-Layer System:

  1. Steel Editorial (owned — Quartr-style premium media)

  2. Member Content (community-created)

  3. Curated Embeds (best of internet, framed with Steel context)

  

B2B Adjacent:

  

- World Sponsorship — brands sponsor world-specific editorial (not banner ads)

- Steel Ventures — investment/operator arm that backs products living natively inside Steel worlds. Concept stage, not operational.

  

Growth Flywheel:

  

World-first content → audience discovers universe → Steel Card real-world viral loop → visible membership creates aspiration → waitlist grows → new members create more world content

  

Punchline: Steel Global is the destination the rest of the ecosystem points toward. The card is the key, the profile is the passport, citizenship is the status, the worlds are the experience.

Legal / Structural
