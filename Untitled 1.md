To disrupt the traditional $100,000 management consulting retainer, post-AI corporate pricing must shift from billing for inputs (hours, headcount) to billing for infrastructure and outputs (capacity, computation, business metrics).

If I were designing the pricing architecture for an AI-native infrastructure firm, I would implement a Value-Capture Hybrid Model structured as follows:

---

## 🧱 1. The Implementation Fee (Cost-Recovery Setup)

Traditional firms charge heavily upfront for PowerPoint decks and discovery. The post-AI model uses automated data ingestion pipelines, drastically reducing discovery costs.

- The Structure: A fixed, low-margin Setup & Deployment Fee.
- The Goal: Cover the compute and engineering sprint costs required to clean internal data pipelines and deploy the core operating system.
- The Pricing: $15,000 – $25,000 (flat fee).

---

## ⚙️ 2. The Core Infrastructure Subscription (SaaS Component)

Instead of paying a monthly consulting retainer to keep humans on standby, the client pays to keep the autonomous AI department running securely.

- The Structure: A tiered monthly subscription based on Operational Data Volume or the number of active Autonomous Agents deployed (e.g., a Finance Agent, a Recruiting Agent).
- The Goal: Cover continuous model fine-tuning, software updates, data sovereignty security, and API hosting.
- The Pricing: $2,500 – $5,000 per month (predictable ARR for the vendor).

---

## ⚡ 3. The Computation and Capacity Metric (Usage Component)

This is where the post-AI paradigm completely breaks away from traditional billing. Instead of billing for "hours worked" by a junior consultant, you bill for digital worker capacity.

- The Structure: A usage-based metric tied to Token/Compute Volume or Task Completion.
- The Goal: Align cost directly with business activity. If the AI processes 10,000 invoices in Q4, the client pays more; if Q1 is slow, they pay less.
- The Pricing: Per 1,000 executed workflows or processed data clusters.

---

## 📈 4. The Value-Share Tracker (The Performance Lever)

Traditional consultants rarely tie their fees to actual financial outcomes. Because post-AI systems are fully digitalized, software can natively track exactly how much money it saves or generates.

- The Structure: A percentage-based Performance Bonus tied to verifiable operational KPIs.
- The Goal: Capture upside while giving the client a zero-risk entry point.
- The Pricing: 5% to 10% of measurable cost reduction (e.g., reduced customer churn or automated vendor auditing) or revenue attribution, capped at a maximum ceiling.

---

## 📊 Consulting vs. Post-AI Pricing Matrix

|Feature|Traditional Management Consulting|Post-AI Infrastructure Model|
|---|---|---|
|Primary Billing Unit|Billable Hours & Headcount|Software Infrastructure & Compute|
|Upfront Cost|$100,000+ (High Risk)|$20,000 (Low Risk Setup)|
|Ongoing Maintenance|Expensive change-orders for human updates|Predictable monthly SaaS subscription|
|Scalability|Costs double if you double the work|Marginal compute cost increases slightly|
|Ownership|Client gets PDFs; firm keeps the expertise|Client owns the autonomous department data|
