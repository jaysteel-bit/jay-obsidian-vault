## Quick Thoughts

Gamify the diffs accumulation. Congratulate users when they pass Flow OS decision thresholds — this drives engagement and makes the AUM base visible to the client.

**Threshold ideas:**
- 100k diffs — "Your operational memory is now smarter than any individual employee"
- 1M diffs — "Flow OS now sees patterns invisible to your team"
- 5M diffs — "Your business is officially self-aware"
- 15M diffs — "Cross-industry prediction engine unlocked"
- 20M diffs — "Foundation model training eligible"

This makes the AUM base tangible. Clients see their "asset" growing. Reinforces the asset management framing — they're accumulating something valuable.
