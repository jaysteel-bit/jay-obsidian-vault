# Alex Hormozi

Entrepreneur, investor, and content creator. Founder of [[Acquisition.com]] (portfolio ~$85M/year revenue). Creator of "Mozi Nation" content brand.

## Key Frameworks (from ingested sources)

- **[[Four Ways to Scale a Service Business]]** — private chain, franchise, licensing, software. The four vehicles for packaging a service business for scale.
- **17 Sales Tips** — referenced in [[Hormozi - 17 Sales Tips]]

## Business Portfolio

Owns businesses in 3 of the 4 scaling models:
- Private chain (12 locations, opening 1/month — transitioned from licensing)
- Licensing (Gym Launch — first business, offer-driven, low capital start)
- Software (lost $3M outsourcing development — warns against it without a technical co-founder)

## Relevant Quotes

- "The franchise is not the end goal. It is the beginning of the next journey."
- "Don't outsource software to a third party team. I promise you it will probably not work. Two million three million dollars of development later I've made the mistakes. Let me give you a three million dollar gift: don't do it."
- On licensing: "You make money throughout the process rather than having a big lump at the end."
