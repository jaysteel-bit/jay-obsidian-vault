### <u>Email Marketing Example:</u>

==Alex Hormozi from Acquisition.com Email:==

**Subject Line:** Mozi Minute: Building a robot content army (II)

**Body Text:** 

Last email I talked about decentralized content - aka - humans making stuff for you at scale.

This one's about replacing the humans entirely.

Ai content creation is exactly what it sounds like. 

You use tools to generate videos, images, captions, and ads without a camera, a crew, or a content calendar.

Here's why it matters right now: the bottleneck in your marketing probably isn't money. 

It's volume. 

You can't make enough content fast enough to feed the algorithm and test every segment. 

An inhouse human team can't either not without a massive payroll. AI can.

There are two ways to do it:

1. Ai affiliate army - 500 accounts of ai influencers who all promote your thing  
    
2. Clone affiliate army - 1 account that posts tons of volume of YOU without you recording, editing, anything

For play 1: You take your best performing hooks, angles, and offers. Then you begin building a different customer avatar for every segment of your customer base. Think every age, gender, race, etc combination. 

Then, have them deliver your best performing content/ad angles.  
  

This is what Medvi (that Ai GLP-1 company that got to $400M in sales in 12 months did). 

For play 2: You take your best performing hooks, angles, and offers. You feed them into Ai tools that clone your voice, your face, your copy style. 

Then you let it output 50 variations. Or 500 variations.  
  
- This is what I’m actively doing. I already have some level of audience, now its about amplifying.

If you do this, some will be terrible. Post them anyway. 

The algorithm will sort it out faster than you will. And the best part, if it sucks, not that many people will see it. 

So, no worries.

The real advantage isn't the content itself. It's the data you get back. 

Every post is a vote. 

Ai lets you run a thousand tests at once instead of one per week. 

Then once you have the winners, you know the best angles and avatars, then you put money behind them and go even bigger & badder.

One warning: garbage in, garbage out. 

Ai amplifies what you give it. 

If your core message is weak, you'll just make bad content faster.

Get the message right first. Then clone it to death.

Next email I'll talk about the third strategy: continuous capture.

Value in under a minute,

- Alex
