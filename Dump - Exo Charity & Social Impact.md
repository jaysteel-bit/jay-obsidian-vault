## Quick Thoughts

**Exo Sub-Mission: Charity**

- Messaging: "What you're doing helps charity in some way"
- Show users on onboarding/signup page that Exo wants to give back to individuals for learning AI — instead of put people out of jobs.
- Core belief: Humans and AI will work together, augmenting human intelligence
- Mission outcome: Making us/teams **Extraordinary** through AI augmentation

## Key Insights

- Social impact is a differentiator.
- Users should understand their participation contributes to something bigger
- AI literacy/education is the charity angle (not generic donations)
- Connects directly to main mission: "Be Extraordinary"
